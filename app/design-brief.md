# Merlin Supply Group LLC website design brief

Design read: A confident, operationally grounded wholesale distributor for U.S. business buyers, manufacturers, and brand partners.
Concept spine: The distribution corridor. Every page uses orderly horizontal lines, clear procurement stages, and warehouse photography to signal a business process that moves from sourcing to order to delivery.
Delivery tier: Editorial corporate website with restrained scroll/hover motion, image movement, and clear responsive navigation.
Locked palette: Deep forest #0B2E27, pine #123D33, warm gold #D2A04A, white #FFFFFF, cool green-gray #F7F8F6 and text #15231F. These reflect the explicitly requested forest green and warm gold corporate brand.
Locked type: Outfit for display and body, with system sans-serif fallback. Geometric sans keeps the business language direct and contemporary.
Animation mode: animated-website (user selected Animated). The image generation service rejected requests because this account requires a higher plan, so the generated 15-second scroll-scrub film could not be created. The current build uses a CSS entrance, hero image movement, card interactions, and smooth anchor navigation; no generated video is wired.
Journey shape: single-shot. Planned journey: (1) warehouse establishing view, (2) aisle sourcing and product inventory, (3) supplier relationship, (4) order documentation, (5) logistics coordination, (6) closing warehouse corridor. One continuous warehouse environment would keep the narrative coherent.
World grammar: Photorealistic U.S. distribution center, clean marked aisles, neutral cartons, daylight industrial fixtures, deep forest green and warm gold visual accents, steady forward movement, no fabricated signage or readable logos.
Mobile framing: all key content left aligned and center-safe, hero crop anchored to aisle center, single-column layouts on narrow viewports, mobile navigation drawer, reduced-motion fallback.
Section plan: hero image with asymmetric copy; three-part value strip; category image cards; split process section; dark partner band with form; separate content pages with split intro, alternating category rows, application form, and policy content.
Asset plan: warehouse, category, office/operations imagery sourced from hosted Unsplash URLs because generated bespoke assets were unavailable at build time. Inline SVG favicon. No invented logo file or unverified warehouse ownership claims.
CTA inventory: Become a brand partner, Open a wholesale account, Explore category, Request category information, Submit wholesale application, Submit brand inquiry, Send message, Contact purchasing.
Operational integrity: Avoid claims of owned warehouse, guaranteed capital strength, universal brand authorization, guaranteed MAP compliance, or live inventory until independently verified. Contact forms prepare an email draft using the visitor's configured mail application; no email delivery backend has been configured.
